FAQ

Driver questions, answered

Will I be home on weekends?

Yes. This is an OTR position: you run during the week and you are home on Saturday and Sunday.

How long does it take to get from Texas to California?

Dallas to Los Angeles is about 1,430 miles, which is roughly 2 days of legal driving. From California, nearby states such as Nevada and Arizona are a same-day run, while Colorado or Washington take about 2 days. Dispatch plans your loads around this so you can be home for the weekend.

What are the 1099 deductions?

This is an independent contractor (1099) position, so no payroll taxes are withheld from your pay. Ask Dave for the full settlement breakdown, including any fuel or equipment charges, and get it in writing before you start.

Where are your yards located?

Trucks are stationed at our yard and we run freight in Texas, California and other states. Dave will give you the exact yard address and pickup time when he calls you after you apply.

How fast do I get paid?

Pay is quoted per week. Dave will confirm the pay day and payment method on your call so there are no surprises.

Do I need my own transportation to the yard?

Yes. Drivers need their own personal transportation to pick up and drop off the truck at the yard.

Do I need a CDL?

No. We hire both CDL Class A drivers (semi, dry van, reefer, flatbed) and non-CDL hotshot drivers. Both need verifiable driving experience and a valid DOT medical card.

Can I bring a pet or a rider?

Pets are allowed. Riders are not allowed.
